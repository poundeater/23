## Current State Summary

Redbelly Community DAO operates under two ratified governing documents, both ratified by Snapshot vote, not by the Google Form process examined separately below.

- Constitution v1.2, ratified via Snapshot vote 20 Sep 2025 ([proposal #2](https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposal/0xcc1ea0e69962dbb390d71a467c0ea69ce0b38de342ec0777e1ef130d11e844e3), "Redbelly Community DAO Constitution - Ratification," 1.8m RBNT voted, 100% For)
- Code of Conduct v1.0, ratified via Snapshot vote 6 Oct 2025 ([proposal #9](https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposal/0x0503ba90c264f15e18cd7ca6b0757bbbdef2b00b0e3d1013f60bd17bc878c94d), "Community Code of Conduct," 92.97% For)

Of the 30 Snapshot proposals on record for rbnt.eth, 18 passed, 6 were rejected, and 6 were closed without a Snapshot-assigned pass or reject label (elections and a small number of votes where the Discord announcement, not Snapshot's own UI, supplied the final call). Governance activity spans 3 Sep 2025 to 24 Jul 2026.

Three findings resolve open items from the prior research pass:

1. The Sep 2025 ratification gap is closed. Constitution v1.2 was ratified via Snapshot proposal #2 on 20 Sep 2025. The 11 to 14 Feb 2026 Google Form ("DAO Constitution Ratification, YES/NO Vote," 9 to 10 respondents) is a separate, later poll and is not how the Constitution was actually ratified. It should not be logged as a ratification event.
2. Guild Structure Consolidation is confirmed. [Snapshot proposal #5](https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposal/0x901e2873e7907d2ee87de5a79beae4fd602e75d053cd9fc6489e97d2547dd039), "DAO Guild Structure, Consolidation," passed on 20 Sep 2025 with 42.95% For against a plurality Abstain. This maps to Constitution Section 3, Working Groups (Pods).
3. No quadratic voting proposal exists anywhere in the 30-proposal Snapshot record. It remains an unimplemented, Discord-only item pending Snapshot evidence.

## Governing Documents

### Constitution v1.2

Full document: [Constitution v1.2, Ratified](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/redbelly-community-dao.firebasestorage.app/o/resources%2F1762476390856-Redbelly%20Community%20DAO%20Constitution%20v1.2%20-%20Ratified.pdf?alt=media&token=c89ccef9-cc7c-4a37-9b43-a3fac9e49ddc)

Version history recorded in the document itself:

- 1.0: Retired
- 1.1: Retired
- 1.2: Ratified via Snapshot vote 20/09/2025

Ten sections: 1. Treasury Overview, 2. Governance Structure, 3. Working Groups (Pods), 4. Governance Process and Proposal Lifecycle, 5. Conflict of Interest Policy, 6. Treasury Management, 7. Contributor Expectations, 8. Community Involvement, 9. Amendments, 10. Founding Principles.

Key mechanics relevant to verifying the changelog below:

- Section 2.3, Voting Power: Phase 0 and 1 use RBNT-weighted voting. Pod Leads may not cast more than one vote per proposal. Conflicted individuals must recuse.
- Section 4.1, Process Overview: submission via Google form, guild review, a 3-day cross-pod Discord feedback window, High Council review, Snapshot vote, Dework onboarding, execution, final review, then payment and public archive. See also the [DAO Proposal Submission Flow Chart](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/redbelly-community-dao.firebasestorage.app/o/resources%2F1762476710083-DAO%20Proposal%20Submission%20Flow%20Chart.pdf?alt=media&token=a035e054-c34c-4158-8402-1a2294ecc7a2).
- Section 9, Amendments: any constitutional change requires 7-day notice, a 5-day voting period open to all DAO members plus High Council, Pod Leaders, and Genesis Leaders, majority token-weighted approval, and public version control.

Organizational context: [DAO Structure Infographic](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/redbelly-community-dao.firebasestorage.app/o/resources%2F1762476859153-DAO%20structure%20Infographic.png?alt=media&token=413f2704-7d76-4193-be24-1643c27b4958) and [DAO Leadership, November 2025](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/redbelly-community-dao.firebasestorage.app/o/resources%2F1762489977538-DAO%20Leadership%20-%20November%202025.pdf?alt=media&token=84fb227b-38c5-4a82-8ac5-18dbf9e01e2b).

## Guild Structure Consolidation: Reasoned Conclusion

Passed and implemented. [Snapshot proposal #5](https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposal/0x901e2873e7907d2ee87de5a79beae4fd602e75d053cd9fc6489e97d2547dd039) ("DAO Guild Structure, Consolidation") closed 20 Sep 2025 with 42.95% For against a 56.71% Abstain plurality and 0.34% Against. It maps directly to Constitution Section 3, Working Groups (Pods), which already reflects a consolidated five-pod structure (Marketing, Builder/Develop, Researcher, Community, Partnerships) with substructures like "Scouts" and "Echo Rangers" explicitly paused, see the [DAO Structure Infographic](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/redbelly-community-dao.firebasestorage.app/o/resources%2F1762476859153-DAO%20structure%20Infographic.png?alt=media&token=413f2704-7d76-4193-be24-1643c27b4958). The high Abstain share (56.71%) means this passed on a simple majority of votes cast rather than strong affirmative consensus, worth noting for anyone treating this as a high-confidence mandate.

## Disputed and Unratified Items

**Feb 2026 Google Form, "DAO Constitution Ratification, YES/NO Vote."** This is not the Constitution's ratification event. The actual ratification was [Snapshot proposal #2](https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposal/0xcc1ea0e69962dbb390d71a467c0ea69ce0b38de342ec0777e1ef130d11e844e3), closed 20 Sep 2025, matching the version history line inside the [Constitution document itself](https://firebasestorage.googleapis.com/v0/b/redbelly-community-dao.firebasestorage.app/o/resources%2F1762476390856-Redbelly%20Community%20DAO%20Constitution%20v1.2%20-%20Ratified.pdf?alt=media&token=c89ccef9-cc7c-4a37-9b43-a3fac9e49ddc). The Google Form is a separate, later, 9 to 10 respondent poll, run through a self-defined 60% adoption threshold that appears nowhere in Constitution Section 9's amendment process (7-day notice, 5-day token-weighted vote). Any changelog entry describing this poll's items as ratified constitutional changes would be inaccurate. If any of its 30 questions correspond to real governance changes, those changes are traceable instead through the Snapshot proposals above, not through this form.

**Constitution v1.3 reference.** [Snapshot proposal #28](https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposal/0xa57ccc9a6d554c9e5a1b1ca429ad2affc2e9058f12ea50bce999460f2aea4e02) (Jul 2026) lists "Redbelly Community DAO Constitution v1.3" as a supporting document. No v1.3 file was present in the supplied repository or in the resource links at the top of this document (constitution, proposals, proposals_links folders, and the five governing-resource links, all point to v1.2 as current). This is an open item: either a v1.3 draft exists elsewhere and was not shared, or the proposal text referenced a document that was not yet formally version-controlled per Section 9's publishing requirement at the time of that vote.

## Unimplemented Cases

**Quadratic voting.** No proposal titled or referencing quadratic voting appears anywhere in the 30-item Snapshot record for rbnt.eth. Section 2.3 of Constitution v1.2 specifies RBNT-weighted voting only. This remains a Discord-only, unimplemented item pending any future Snapshot submission.

**Whitelist requirement.** This one is resolved, not unimplemented. [Snapshot proposal #28](https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposal/0xa57ccc9a6d554c9e5a1b1ca429ad2affc2e9058f12ea50bce999460f2aea4e02) passed in Jul 2026 and explicitly removes the whitelist requirement for general DAO voting participation while retaining geographic limitations for High Council and Leadership roles, matching the "voter eligibility opened to all RBNT holders" finding from the Discord export dated to early Jul 2026.

## Data Gaps

- Exact vote-choice percentages were not visible in the supplied PDF screenshots for proposals 12, 13, 15, 16, 17, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29 (the pass or reject outcome is known from Snapshot's own status label, but the margin is not). If precise margins matter for the final deliverable, these would need re-export from Snapshot with the RESULTS sidebar visible.
- Precise Created and End timestamps are confirmed for proposals 1 through 11 and 30. For proposals 12 through 29, only relative age labels ("Xmo ago" as of the 15 Aug 2026 capture date) were available, giving month-level rather than day-level precision.
- The Constitution v1.3 document referenced in proposal 28 was not located.
- Proposal 10 (DAO logo vote) and proposal 18 and 30 (elections) are multi-choice or ranked votes rather than For/Against/Abstain proposals, so "Passed/Rejected" framing does not apply to them; they are reported as winner-by-power instead.
