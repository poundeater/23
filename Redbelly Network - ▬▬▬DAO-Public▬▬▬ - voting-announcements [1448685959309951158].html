That means: Your voting power is proportional to your RBNT holdings. You can allocate more or less of your voting weight to the option you choose. You are not limited to a simple binary mindset — conviction matters. For example: You may use 100% of your voting power if you strongly support or oppose a proposal. Or you may use only part of your voting weight if your conviction is moderate. This allows governance to reflect intensity of belief, not just direction. Please vote responsibly and in alignment with the long-term interests of the Redbelly ecosystem. Governance is not just participation — it is structured decision-making.

Buffy_NFTs 21-07-2026 06:45
Note for @everyone: The High Council vote has been reset with a different voting method. Since our first election, ranked voting in Snapshot no longer produces the desired results. Ranked voting now uses ranked choice (instant runoff), which is designed to elect a single winner. Since we are filling 3 High Council seats, that method could not produce the right outcome, which is why some of you saw rankings behaving unexpectedly. The new proposal uses Copeland Voting which also allows you to rank your choices in the order you want to see them elected. Simple and transparent. Apologies for the reset. Please cast your votes again on the new proposal here: https://snapshot.box/#/s:rbnt.eth/proposals (edited)
